Beneficiary Registry & Profiling

Centralized Social Welfare Records

Beneficiary Enrollment

Digitally onboard indigent families, senior citizens, PWDs, solo parents, and other priority sectors with complete household profiling and socio-economic data.

Digital Beneficiary ID

Issue unique beneficiary IDs with embedded QR codes for fast identity verification during aid releases and program enrollments.

Household Mapping

Link individual beneficiaries to household units. Track all members, their status, and participation in social programs across the family structure.

Category Classification

Classify beneficiaries by sector: 4Ps, Senior Citizens, PWDs, Solo Parents, Street Dwellers, AICS Recipients, and custom LGU-defined categories.

Smart Search & Filter

Instantly locate any beneficiary by name, barangay, category, or ID. Advanced filters for generating targeted lists for specific program distributions.

Aid History Ledger

Complete chronological record of every assistance received per beneficiary: type, date, amount, and releasing officer — ensuring full accountability.

Aid Distribution & Program Management

Social Assistance Workflows

AICS Management

Manage Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation requests — intake, assessment, recommendation, and release — with digital case notes per client.

Distribution Events

Schedule and manage aid distribution activities. Generate master lists, track attendance, and record actual releases in real time.

Duplicate Prevention

Automatic detection of duplicate applications or double-claiming beneficiaries across different programs within the same distribution period.

Social Case Records

Manage individual case files for clients requiring intervention. Social workers record case notes, referrals, and case closure status digitally.

Scholarship Tracking

Track CSWD-administered scholarship grantees, disbursement schedules, academic requirements, and renewal status per scholar.

Reporting & Welfare Analytics

Data-Driven Social Development

Sector Dashboard

Visual breakdowns of beneficiary population by sector, barangay, age group, and economic status for planning and budgeting.

DSWD Reports

Auto-generate DSWD-required reports (AICS Utilization, 4Ps Compliance, Social Protection Statistics) from live system data.

Barangay Heatmap

Geographic visualization of beneficiary density and aid distribution across barangays to identify underserved areas.

Audit Trail

Immutable log of all data entries, edits, and releases with timestamps and responsible officer — meeting COA audit requirements.
