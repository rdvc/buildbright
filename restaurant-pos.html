Front-of-House & Order Management

Table Service & Billing Operations

Visual Floor Map

Dynamic table mapping with real-time status indicators — Available, Occupied, Dirty, and Reserved — updated instantly as service progresses.

Tableside Ordering

Direct order entry via tablets instantly routes orders to kitchen printers or display screens. Eliminates kitchen slips and reduces order errors to zero.

Smart Billing

Effortlessly split bills by item, seat, or percentage. Supports GCash, Maya, Card, and Cash payments with automatic OR generation.

Kitchen Display (KDS)

Digital kitchen ticket display for chefs with built-in timers and priority alerts to reduce preparation times and prevent order backlog.

Tax & Discounts

Automated Senior Citizen, PWD, and promotional discount calculations. Full audit log of all applied discounts for BIR and tax compliance reporting.

Z-Reading Reports

Generate daily sales summaries, VAT reports, and staff performance analytics with one click. Exportable to Excel and print-ready PDF formats.

Inventory & Kitchen Operations

Stock Management & Production Tracking

Ingredient Tracking

Recipe-based inventory deductions. Tracks every gram of stock used in real-time whenever an order is sent to the kitchen.

Low Stock Alerts

Automated alerts when ingredient levels fall below minimum thresholds. Prevents 86-ing menu items during peak service hours.

Offline Sync

Keep taking orders even if the internet fails. Data automatically syncs with the central server once connectivity is restored.

Distributed Printing

Print to specific kitchen or bar stations simultaneously. ESC/POS printer support for thermal receipt and kitchen ticket printers.

Role-Based Access

Separate permission tiers for Cashier, Waiter, Chef, and Manager. Secure audit trail tracks every voided item or modified transaction.
