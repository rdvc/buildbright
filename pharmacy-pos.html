Drug Management & Sales Processing

Generic Substitution

Search by generic name to find equivalent brands instantly. Help customers access affordable medication with accurate alternatives from your stock.

FEFO Inventory

First-Expiry, First-Out (FEFO) logic ensures that oldest stock is always sold first, minimizing waste and preventing expired medication from reaching customers.

Retail & Wholesale Pricing

Flexible pricing for single tablets, strips, or full boxes. Automatically recalculates inventory units based on the packaging sold at the counter.

Expiration Alerts

Automated dashboard notifications for items nearing their 30, 60, or 90-day expiration window. Prevents expired stock from remaining on shelves.

SC & PWD Discounts

Standardized calculation for Senior Citizens and PWD discounts with full audit logs for tax reporting and DSWD compliance documentation.

FDA-Ready Reports

Generate mandated reports for dangerous drugs and sensitive inventory items with a single click, formatted to regulatory requirements.

Supplier Management

Track purchase orders and restock history. Maintain detailed relationship logs with distributors and monitor delivery timelines.

Profit Analytics

Detailed reports on cost vs. retail price per item. Understand your pharmacy's real-time margin by product, category, and supplier.
