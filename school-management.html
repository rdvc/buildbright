Enrollment, Grading & Attendance

Digital Enrollment

Automated admission process with document uploading and student profile creation. Reduces physical queues and eliminates paper forms entirely.

Attendance Tracking

Track daily student attendance with automated SMS notifications for parents when a student is absent. Generates monthly attendance reports per class.

Automated Grading

Easy grade encoding for teachers with automatic GWA computation and class ranking. Generates printable report cards formatted to DepEd standards.

Financial Dashboard

Manage tuition fees, miscellaneous charges, and scholarship grants with full payment history, balance tracking, and official receipt generation.

Faculty Portal

Dedicated space for teachers to manage class schedules, upload lesson plans, encode grades, and monitor student performance metrics.

Library Management

Digital book cataloging with borrower tracking, return date monitoring, and automated fine calculations for overdue materials.

Student Portal

Secure student login to view grades, download study materials, check financial balance, and track enrollment status from any device.

Certificate Generation

One-click generation of Certificates of Enrollment, Good Moral, and Transcript of Records (TOR) with official school letterhead.
