Intelligent Document Tracking

Real-Time Monitoring & QR Routing

Integrated QR Logic

Uses high-speed scanner API to process arrivals and departures with a single beep. Compatible with MP7200 hardware for seamless office integration.

Movement Timeline

Visual Shopee-style representation of a document's journey from Origin to Final Release, showing timestamps and handlers at every station.

Office Accountability

Automatically identifies the logger's office upon login to ensure accurate receiving records and full chain-of-custody documentation.

Automated Reports

Generate daily or monthly movement reports with printable audit trails for transparency compliance and internal accountability reviews.

Role-Based Access

Secure authentication for Administrators, Dept. Heads, and Office Staff with specific permission tiers to control data access.

Live Dashboard Updates

Dashboard updates in real-time as documents are scanned across different building terminals, providing instant office-wide visibility.

Archive & Document Management

Filing, Retrieval & Classification

Document Classification

Categorize incoming documents by type, originating office, urgency, and routing destination for organized processing.

Instant Retrieval

Search any document by tracking number, title, originating office, or date. Complex indexing for sub-second retrieval from archive.

SLA & TAT Monitoring

Track turnaround time per document and office. Flag documents exceeding SLA thresholds for administrative intervention.

Digital Routing Slips

Replace paper routing slips with digital forwarding. Each transfer is logged with receiver name, time, and digital acknowledgment.

Release Confirmation

Require digital acknowledgment from receiving parties before marking a document as released or closed in the system.
