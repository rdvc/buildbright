Hospital Management

A health information system for diagnostic clinics, hospitals and city health offices. It covers pre-employment medical exams (PEME) from start to finish: thumbprint registration, laboratory, physical and neuro exam, fitness classification and a printed medical certificate, plus pharmacy, billing and reports.

Registration & Biometrics

Patient Identity You Can Trust

Patient Registration & EMR

Demographics, photo, allergies and conditions under a unique Patient ID (P-######), with a full visit timeline for every patient.

Thumbprint Capture

Right and left thumbprints from a DigitalPersona or SecuGen scanner, or by image upload, with recorded consent. An exemption reason can be logged when no thumb can be captured.

Identity Verification

The examining physician can match the person in front of them against the stored thumbprint, so no one else can take the exam for the applicant.

Walk-in Queue & Appointments

Walk-ins get a queue number and scheduled patients are checked in. Each exam visit gets a Visit ID (V-YYYY-######) and records the position applied for and the hiring hospital.

Lab Packages per Hospital

Named sets of lab tests linked to each hiring hospital. The right package is selected automatically and sent to the laboratory in one click.

Multi-Facility Branding

The facility name, logo, address and contact are set in Settings, so any clinic or hospital can run the system under its own name.

02 / 04

Laboratory & Medical Exam

From Specimen to Physician Findings

Laboratory Workflow

Each request moves through Requested, Collected, Processing, For Verification and Released, then goes back to the doctor for review.

Diagnostic Tests

Chest X-ray, ECG, drug test, HBsAg, RPR and other tests have their readings recorded, verified and released like any other lab result.

Vital Signs & Screening

Vital signs with automatic BMI, plus vision and hearing tests, captured at the start of the medical exam.

17-System Physical Exam

A structured head-to-toe physical examination with findings, procedures and the physician's notes in one screen.

Neuro Test

Mental status and neurological examination recorded as part of the exam, with its own fee.

Safe Prescribing

Prescriptions are checked against the patient's allergies, including basic drug-class cross-sensitivity, before they are saved.

03 / 04

Certification, Pharmacy & Billing

Results, Medicines and Payments

Fitness Classification

The physician assigns Class A, B, C, Pending or Unfit. Only Pending can be finalized while lab results are still out.

Medical Certificates

Finalizing an exam issues a certificate number (MC-YYYY-######) and prints a certificate with the applicant's photo and thumbprint. Corrections are amendments with a reason.

Pharmacy & Inventory

Batch tracking and dispensing, with a red alarm for stock that expires within 60 days and one-click write-off of expired batches.

Smart Medicine Encoding

Suggests generic names, brands, strengths and suppliers encoded before, cleans up spelling, and refuses to save duplicate medicines.

Billing & Free-Services Mode

Charges post automatically for exams, labs, medicines and procedures. Government facilities can switch to free-services mode, which hides all prices and payment screens.

Reports & Analytics

Patients served by age group, sex and barangay, applicants per lab package, and revenue reports when billing is on.

04 / 04

Security & Administration

Accountable, Compliant Records

Role-Based Access

Module access by role for doctors, nurses, front desk, lab, pharmacy and billing, with per-user overrides that take effect on the next page load.

Versioned Clinical Records

Finalized consultations and released results are never overwritten. Every edit needs a reason and the earlier version is kept.

Audit Trail

Every action is logged with the user, time, record and before/after values, including thumbprint captures and verifications.

Hardened Security

Bcrypt passwords, account lockout, session timeout, CSRF tokens, prepared statements and protected storage for biometric images.

Backups

SQL backups can be downloaded from Settings at any time.

Built-in User Manual

An illustrated user manual that staff can open from the account menu.
