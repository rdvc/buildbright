Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Build Bright Software Solution ("BuildBright", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website www.buildbrightsoftware.com, our Facebook Page, and the tools we use to publish content, and how we handle it in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use our contact form, email us, call or message us, we receive what you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, organization and message.
  • Facebook Page. When you interact with our Facebook Page (likes, comments, messages), Facebook processes that information under its own policies. We see what Facebook shows to Page administrators.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry, arrange demos and provide quotations.
  • To deliver, support and improve the systems we build for you.
  • To keep our website and services secure.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Our Facebook publishing app

We use our own app, BuildBright Poster, only to publish posts (images, videos and captions) to the BuildBright Software Solutions Facebook Page that we manage. The app does not collect, store or process personal data of Facebook users. The only access it holds is a Page access token for our own Page, which is stored securely on our own computer.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers we need to run our business (for example, email and web hosting), when required by law, or with your consent.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to its processing. Contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of any data related to your interactions with us, including through Facebook, email buildbrightsoftwaresolution@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will confirm once it is done.
