Centralized Identity Management

Admin Control & Resident Registry

Centralized Admin

Real-time control panel to monitor all registered residents, pending applications, and overall system status — giving city officers a single source of truth.

Resident Records

Advanced management tools to search, update, and verify resident identities securely. Maintain demographic data with full update history per resident.

Application Flow

Streamlined workflow to review, approve, or reject ID applications submitted by residents. Track each application from submission to card release.

Print Management

Efficiently manage approved IDs in the printing queue with status monitoring — from layout generation to card production and distribution.

QR Validation

Fast identity validation by scanning resident ID QR codes during public transactions. Real-time lookup against the central resident database.

City Analytics

Detailed statistics on enrollment growth, demographic breakdowns, and registration trends to support data-driven city planning decisions.

AES Encryption

High-level data encryption ensures compliance with national privacy and security laws. All personal data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Live Support Chat

Integrated real-time chat for residents to report issues or ask questions directly to the admin team without leaving the portal.
