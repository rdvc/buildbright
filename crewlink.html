Fleet & Crew Management

Streamlining Maritime Operations

Fleet Intelligence Dashboard

Real-time Fleet Intelligence reports providing an instant snapshot of active sessions, sailing crew count, deployment readiness, and vessel status across the fleet.

Visa & Document Alerts

Automated tracking of crew documentation with high-priority alerts for visas, certifications, and sea service records expiring within 6 months.

Vessel Management

Comprehensive tracking of fleet status, categorizing vessels by Active Deployment, Maintenance, or Inactive — with assigned crew manifests per vessel.

Personnel Roster

Centralized database for managing crew profiles, embarkation history, payroll status, and Onsigner/Offsigner rotation status for every fleet member.

PVC ID Module

Integrated tool to generate professional maritime ID cards with dynamic QR codes for secure port verification and authorized ship boarding.

Access Monitoring

Detailed audit logs tracking every system access event — maintaining high-level security and full administrative accountability across shore-based offices.
