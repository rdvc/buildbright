Patient & Appointment Management

Modernizing Healthcare Access

Patient Dashboard

Personalized overview for registered patients to manage bookings, track upcoming schedules, and search for healthcare providers in one unified view.

Doctor & Session Search

Advanced filtering to find healthcare professionals by specialty, profile, or available consultation time slots. View doctor availability in real-time.

Online Booking

Patients select and confirm appointment slots digitally, eliminating unnecessary physical visits for scheduling. Conflict-prevention logic avoids double-booking.

Doctor Directory

Complete access to healthcare professional profiles, specialties, clinic hours, and real-time availability for effective patient-provider matching.

Facility Admin Panel

Live tracking of total registered patients, appointment volume, and booking trends for efficient clinic or health center administration.

Secure Patient Privacy

Encrypted login protocols and role-based access controls ensuring that sensitive medical schedules and patient records remain strictly confidential.
