Clinical Operations & Patient Flow

Digitalizing Healthcare Delivery

Patient Registration & EMR

Unique patient IDs, full profiling, allergies and conditions, with a complete visit timeline. Clinical records are versioned, never overwritten.

Appointments, Queue & Triage

Handle scheduled and walk-in patients with queue numbers, then capture vital signs and triage priority before consultation.

Doctor Consultation

SOAP notes, diagnosis, lab orders, prescriptions and disposition in one screen, with allergy and drug-class cross-sensitivity checks.

Laboratory Workflow

Track every request from collection and processing through verification and release, with results routed back for doctor review.

Pharmacy & Inventory

FEFO dispensing, batch tracking and a 60-day expiry alarm, with smart encoding that suggests medicines and blocks duplicates.

Admissions & Billing

Bed management, nursing notes and discharge, plus automatic charge posting. Switch to free-services mode for government facilities.

Role-Based Access

Per-role and per-user module access for doctors, nurses, front desk, lab, pharmacy and billing staff.

Audit Trail

Every action is logged with user, time and before/after values for full accountability and compliance.

Reports & Analytics

Patients served by age group, sex and barangay, plus revenue reports when billing is enabled.
