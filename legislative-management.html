Legislative Records & Filing

Ordinances, Resolutions & Measures

Ordinance Management

File, index, and archive city or municipal ordinances with full revision history. Each ordinance is stamped, numbered automatically, and linked to the sponsoring councilor.

Resolution Tracking

Monitor the lifecycle of every resolution — from first reading to final approval or disapproval — with full audit trails and councilor votes on record.

Referral & Endorsement

Digitally route measures to standing committees with timestamps. Track endorsements, returns, and final actions at each legislative stage.

Legislative Search Engine

Instantly retrieve any ordinance or resolution by number, title, keyword, or sponsoring legislator. Full-text search across the entire legislative archive.

Auto-Generated Documents

Automatically produce formatted ordinance templates, committee reports, and official transmittal letters with pre-filled data from the system database.

Version Control

Maintain complete amendment history for every measure. Compare versions side by side and restore previous drafts as needed.

Session & Plenary Management

Meeting Records & Attendance

Session Scheduler

Plan regular, special, and emergency sessions with automatic agenda generation from pending measures.

Agenda Builder

Drag-and-drop agenda creation for each session. Automatically attach relevant documents and assign time slots per item.

Attendance & Quorum

Digital roll call and automatic quorum checking. Records absences, late arrivals, and proxies for official minutes.

Voting Records

Capture and archive Ayes, Nayes, and Abstentions per measure. Generate official vote tallies for the minutes.

Minutes Generator

Auto-compile session minutes from attendance, agenda, and vote records. Export to print-ready PDF with official letterhead.

Committee & Councilor Portal

Profiles, Committees & Accountability

Councilor Profiles

Complete digital dossier: committee memberships, authored measures, attendance rate, and term history.

Committee Tracker

Manage standing and special committees, their memberships, scheduled meetings, and pending referrals.

Performance Dashboard

Visual analytics on legislative output: ordinances passed per term, session attendance trends, and committee productivity.

Notifications

Alert councilors of upcoming sessions, pending committee reports, and measures requiring their action or signature.
