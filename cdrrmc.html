Emergency Command Center

An emergency response and disaster command system for City and Municipal DRRM offices. Residents send SOS from an app or by SMS, the Command Center triages it, the nearest available ambulance, fire, rescue or police unit is dispatched automatically, and every unit is tracked live on the map until the case is closed.

Command Center Console

Real-Time Incident Command

Live Board & Map

Every open incident, unit and resident location on one live map (OpenStreetMap), refreshed every few seconds or pushed instantly over WebSockets.

Priority Triage & Alarms

Incidents are classed as Critical, Urgent or Advisory. Critical raises a full-screen flashing alarm and siren that stays up until someone acknowledges it.

Automatic Dispatch

Picks the nearest available unit of the right type and alerts the crew at once. The dispatcher has a 15-second window to approve, swap, add a unit or stand it down.

Incident Queueing

When no unit is free, the incident is queued and the next matching unit that frees up is sent to it automatically.

Live Tracking Trails

While an SOS is open, the resident's GPS updates every 5 seconds and is drawn as a trail next to the responding crews.

Training Simulator

Inject app and SMS SOS calls, drive units toward incidents and return them to base, for dispatcher and crew drills.

02 / 04

Resident Emergency App

Help in One Tap, Even Offline

Citizen Emergency ID

Residents sign up with a mobile OTP and a profile with medical info, barangay, home pin and emergency contact, and get an Emergency ID with a QR code.

One-Tap SOS

A 3-second cancel countdown while GPS warms up, then the SOS is sent with the resident's exact location and medical profile.

Offline SMS SOS

With no internet, the app sends a coded SOS text instead. Basic phones can text SOS too. Duplicate SOS from app and SMS merge into one ticket.

Incident Reports with Photos

Residents report floods, fires, road hazards and other incidents with a photo and location.

Hotline Quick-Dial

Emergency hotlines sync from the server and can be saved to the phone's contacts in one tap.

I Am Safe / Need Evacuation

During a disaster, residents check in as safe or ask for evacuation. Evacuation requests become urgent incidents.

03 / 04

Responder Crew App

Field Units Always in Sync

Instant Assignment Alerts

Ambulance, fire, rescue, boat and police crews are alerted the moment they are assigned, on a phone or tablet.

Status Lifecycle

Crews move through En route, On scene, Transporting, At hospital and Completed. The last unit to complete closes the case.

Live Unit GPS

Each unit's position is shared live with the Command Center, and units that go silent are marked offline.

Hospital Transport

Crews pick the receiving hospital during transport so the Command Center knows where every patient is going.

Multi-Unit Response

Several units can respond to one incident, each with its own status and route.

Android App

A dedicated Android app with native GPS, so location works reliably in the field.

04 / 04

Disaster Watch & Safeguards

City-Wide Alerts and Abuse Control

Disaster Watch Alerts

Send alerts to all residents, to high-risk barangays or to selected barangays, and track who has checked in.

Prank Control (3 Strikes)

False alarms count against the caller's mobile number. Warnings go out at strikes 1 and 2, and the number is blocked automatically on the 3rd. Only an administrator can unblock it.

SMS Gateway

Verification codes and offline SOS through Semaphore or an Android SMS gateway phone, with an inbox and outbox log in the console.

Barangay Boundaries

Official barangay boundaries can be imported so incidents are matched to the right barangay automatically.

Secure Sign-in

One account per mobile number, hashed 6-digit codes with limited tries and resends, and role-based access for admins, dispatchers and crews.

Open REST API

A versioned API for future mobile apps and partner integrations.
