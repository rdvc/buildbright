Resident & Household Management

Digitalizing the Barangay

Resident Profile Management

Centralized database for registering profiles, demographic data, and contact details. Full search, update, and secure verification tools for barangay staff.

Family Registry

Groups residents by household units to track family structures and relationships — essential for disaster response planning and relief distribution.

Digital ID Generation

Create printable Barangay IDs with resident photos, QR code, and official barangay seal for secure inhabitant identification at checkpoints.

Barangay Clearance

Quick issuance of clearances with automated record-tracking. Maintains a full digital history of every clearance issued per resident for compliance.

Indigent Identification

Identify residents qualified for social assistance, relief distribution, and welfare monitoring effectively with tagged indigent household profiles.

Business Management

Process and monitor Business Clearances within the barangay, tracking owner information and compliance for all local establishments.

Blotter Case Recording

Official digital logbook for recording incidents and disputes with detailed status tracking and resolution notes for the Lupon Tagapamayapa.

Reporting & Analytics

Generate statistical data on population, clearances, and blotter cases to help barangay officials make data-driven decisions for the community.
