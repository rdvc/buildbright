Property & Land Record Management

Visualizing Property Data

GIS Dashboard

Centralized view for monitoring mapping progress, showing the ratio of mapped vs. unmapped property data points across the entire jurisdiction.

Property Registry

Secure database for managing buildings and land parcels, tracking usage, ownership, and maintaining precise GPS coordinates for each property.

Tax Declaration Tracking

Integrated module for managing Tax Declarations with direct digital file associations, timestamped history, and payment status monitoring.

Owner Mapping

Registry that links multiple land parcels to a single individual or corporate entity for consolidated ownership lookup and tax assessment.

Interactive Map Preview

Live spatial window that loads property data points on a digital map for rapid visual verification of land boundaries and adjacent parcel relationships.

Document Upload Logs

Real-time activity feed of recent property document uploads, allowing administrators to monitor new entries and verify completeness instantly.
