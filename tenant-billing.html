Billing & Collection Management

Rent, Utilities & Payment Processing

Tenant Profiling

Maintain detailed records of tenants including contract terms, security deposits, move-in date, and valid identification documents for each unit.

Utility Metering

Input current electricity and water meter readings. The system automatically calculates consumption and costs based on your configured rates per unit.

Automated Due Dates

Set recurring monthly rent for each tenant. The system automatically generates new billing statements on your preferred cut-off dates every month.

Arrears Tracking

Instantly see all tenants with overdue balances. The system tracks unpaid amounts and automatically carries them forward to the next billing cycle.

E-Invoices

Generate clean, printable PDF invoices with itemized utility and rent breakdown. Send billing summaries via SMS or email directly to tenants.

Payment Collection

Record partial or full payments with support for cash, bank transfers, and e-wallets. Issue digital acknowledgment receipts for every transaction.

Occupancy Monitoring

Visual dashboard showing which units are Occupied, Vacant, or Under Maintenance — helping you plan renovations and market available spaces effectively.

Collection Analytics

View monthly income reports, collection efficiency rates, and projected revenue for coming months to make informed property management decisions.
